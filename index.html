01 — Approach

Good design is not decoration. It is the shortest path between a person and what they came to do — built once, documented properly, and beautiful enough that the team defends it.

A partner in the room, not a supplier at the gate.

I work as an embedded partner rather than a vendor. That means sitting in the messy part of the problem with your team, pressure-testing the strategy before a single frame is drawn, and staying through implementation so the thing that ships is the thing we designed.

Twelve years across fintech, healthcare and commerce taught me that the hard part is rarely the pixels. It is the alignment: naming the tradeoffs out loud, making the invisible system legible, and giving engineers a spec they actually want to build.

— Melanie

04 — Process

Four moves, repeated until it ships

No black boxes and no three-week silences. You will always know what I am working on and what I need from you next.

  1. 01

    Orient

    A week inside your problem. Stakeholder interviews, a teardown of what exists, and an honest map of the constraints nobody wrote down.

  2. 02

    Frame

    We agree on the one thing this project must be true about. Everything downstream gets measured against it — including my own ideas.

  3. 03

    Design

    Weekly working sessions, not big reveals. You see rough thinking early and polished work late, so the direction is never a surprise.

  4. 04

    Ship

    I stay through build. Specs, tokens, QA passes against real code, and a handover document your next designer will thank you for.
